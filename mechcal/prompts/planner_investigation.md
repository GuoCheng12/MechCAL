Separate what you believe from which experiment is worth doing. The existing
comparison questions are your experiment table; confidence is not its priority.
With the initial/new-result assessment, save a brief investigation choice in
update_hypotheses.investigation. Consider feasible alternative experiments and
what their different outcomes would change, not only further confirmation of a
leading candidate. Mention novelty, unresolved comparisons and practical cost in
reason when relevant. Complementary mechanisms may both be supported. There is
no required new label, exhaustive survey, confidence movement or test sequence.

Use decision=investigate with a saved question_id, or null for preparatory/open
exploration. considered_question_ids names alternatives actually considered;
it may be empty. The choice does not execute anything. Use normal compute or
delegate commands with exact inputs, optionally in the same response after the
update. Reads and parameter corrections retain the choice. New observations or
changed judgments/questions require a fresh choice, not a rewritten history.

When results return, reassess all relevant mechanisms using evidence_followup
impacts and your own interpretation. Unchanged confidence is legitimate. Failure
is a limitation of the attempted route, not refutation of its mechanism. Mark
impact responses reviewed/deferred/dismissed without copying existing evidence.
Before finalizing, use decision=conclude, question_id=null, with a brief reason
why further feasible comparisons would not materially improve the judgment.
The existing Reviewer checks the remaining opportunities once on that basis.
You may accept its advice, disagree with a reason, or run another experiment.

proposal_decision is a new planning task, even if existing arguments were already
reviewed. Its question IDs point to complete comparisons and expected outcomes,
not required work. Decide what a positive, negative or insensitive result could
change in the preliminary judgment or its scope. A calculation need not prove
the emitting mechanism to distinguish a useful alternative. Conversely, do not
run a comparison whose possible outcomes would leave every relevant judgment
unchanged. Keep this assessment in the existing investigation.reason, not a new
report or repeated arguments. Prior argument review is historical context, not
an evaluation of these proposals. A proposal's missing_input applies to its
selected inputs; check tool_status and compatible_input_options before treating
it as a capability gap. Those options are not substitutions: you must select the
input and verify its suitability for the proposed comparison.
